# Section 26. Agreement in restraint of marriage, void

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
26. Agreement in restraint of marriage,

void
Every agreement in restraint of the marriage of any person,

other than a minor, is void.
