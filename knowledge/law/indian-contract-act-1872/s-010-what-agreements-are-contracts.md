# Section 10. What agreements are contracts

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
10. What agreements are contracts 
All agreements are contracts if they are made by the free

consent of parties competent to contract, for a lawful consideration and with a

lawful object, and are not hereby expressly declared to be void.
Nothing herein contained shall affect any law in force in 3[India],

and not hereby expressly repealed, by which any contract is required to be made

in writing  or in the presence of witnesses, or any law

relating to the registration of documents.
