# Section 41. Effect of accepting performance from third person

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
41. Effect of accepting performance

from third person
When a promisee accepts performance of the promise from a third

person, he cannot afterwards enforce it against the promisor.
