# Section 8. Acceptance by performing conditions, or receiving consideration

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
8. Acceptance by performing conditions,

or receiving consideration
Performance of the conditions of a proposal, for the acceptance

of any consideration for a reciprocal promise which may be offered with a

proposal, is an acceptance of the proposal.
