# Section 31. "Contingent contract" defined

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
31. "Contingent contract"

defined 
A "contingent contract" is a contract to do or not to

do something, if some event, collateral to such contract, does or does not

happen.
 Illustration 
A contracts to pay to B Rs. 10,000 if B's house is burnt. This

is a contingent contract.
