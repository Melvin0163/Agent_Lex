# Section 1. Short title

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
1. Short title
This Act may be called the Indian Contract Act, 1872.
Extent, commencement-It extends to the whole of India 1[except

the State of Jammu and Kashmir]; and it shall come into force on the first day

of September, 1872.
Enactments repealed- 2[ * * *] Nothing herein

contained shall affect the provisions of any Statute, Act or Regulation not

hereby expressly repealed, nor any usage or custom or trade, nor any incident

of any contract, not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act.
