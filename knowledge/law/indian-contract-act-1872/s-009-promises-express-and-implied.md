# Section 9. Promises, express and implied

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
9. Promises, express and implied
In so far as the proposal or acceptance of any promise is made

in words, the promise is said to be express. In so far as such proposal or

acceptance is made otherwise than in words, the promise is said to be implied.
