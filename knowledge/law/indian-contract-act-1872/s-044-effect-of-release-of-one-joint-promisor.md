# Section 44. Effect of release of one joint promisor

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
44. Effect of release of one joint

promisor
Where two or more persons have made a joint promise, a release

of one of such joint promisors by the promisee does not discharge the other

joint promisor or joint promisors, neither does it free the joint promisor so

released from responsibility to the other joint promisor or joint promisors.
