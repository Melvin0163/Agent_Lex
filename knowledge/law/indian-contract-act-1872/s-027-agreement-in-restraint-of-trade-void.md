# Section 27. Agreement in restraint of trade, void

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
27. Agreement in restraint of trade,

void
Every agreement by which anyone is restrained from exercising a

lawful profession, trade or business of any kind, is to that extent void.
 Exception 1 :  Saving of agreement not to carry on business

of which goodwill is sold-One who sells the goodwill of a business may agree

with the buyer to refrain from carrying on a similar business, within specified

local limits, so long as the buyer, or any person deriving title to the

goodwill from him, carries on a like business therein, provided that such

limits appear to the court reasonable, regard being had to the nature of the

business.
