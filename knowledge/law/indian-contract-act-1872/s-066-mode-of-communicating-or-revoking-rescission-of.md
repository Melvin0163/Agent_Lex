# Section 66. Mode of communicating or revoking rescission of voidable contract

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
66. Mode of communicating or revoking

rescission of voidable contract 
The rescission of a voidable contract may be communicated or

revoked in the same manner, and subject to the same rules, as apply to the

communication or revocation of the proposal.
