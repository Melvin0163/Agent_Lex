# Section 22. Contract caused by mistake of one party as to matter of fact

Part of [Indian Contract Act, 1872](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

Indian Contract Act, 1872 
22. Contract caused by mistake of one

party as to matter of fact
A contract is not voidable merely because it was caused by one

of the parties to it being under a mistake as to a matter of fact.
