# Section 65. Power of Central Government to give directions

Part of [Code on Wages, 2019](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

The Code on Wages, 2019
65. Power of Central Government to give directions.-
The Central Government may, for carrying into execution of the provisions of this Code in the State give directions to the State Government, and the State Government shall abide by such directions.
