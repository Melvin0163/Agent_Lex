# Section 66. Saving

Part of [Code on Wages, 2019](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

The Code on Wages, 2019
66. Saving.-
Nothing contained in this Code shall be deemed to affect the provisions of the Mahatma Gandhi National Rural Employment Guarantee Act, 2005 (42 of 2005) and the Coal Mines Provident Fund and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1948 (46 of 1948), or of any scheme made there under.
