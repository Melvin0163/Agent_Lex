# Section 57. Bar of suits

Part of [Code on Wages, 2019](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

The Code on Wages, 2019
Chapter X
Miscellaneous
57. Bar of suits.-
No court shall entertain any suit for the recovery of minimum wages, any deduction from wages, discrimination in wages and payment of bonus, in so far as the sum so claimed-
(a) forms the subject of claims under section 45;
(b) has formed the subject of a direction under this Code;
(c) has been adjudged in any proceeding under this Code;
(d) could have been recovered under this Code.
