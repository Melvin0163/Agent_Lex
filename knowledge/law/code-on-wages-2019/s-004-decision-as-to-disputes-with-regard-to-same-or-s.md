# Section 4. Decision as to disputes with regard to same or similar nature of work

Part of [Code on Wages, 2019](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

The Code on Wages, 2019
4. Decision as to disputes with regard to same or similar nature of work.-
Where there is any dispute as to whether a work is of same or similar nature for the purposes of section 3, the dispute shall be decided by such authority as may be notified by the appropriate Government.
