# Section 14. Power to exempt

Part of [Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
14. Power to exempt
The appropriate government may, by notification in the Official

Gazette, exempt, conditionally or unconditionally, any industrial establishment

or class of industrial establishments from all or any of the provisions of this

Act.
