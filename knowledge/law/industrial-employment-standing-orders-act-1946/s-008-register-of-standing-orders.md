# Section 8. Register of standing orders

Part of [Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
8. Register of standing orders
A copy of all standing orders as finally certified under this

Act shall be field by the Certifying Officer in a register in the prescribed

form maintained for the purpose, and the Certifying Officer shall furnish a

copy thereof to any person applying therefor on payment of the prescribed fee.
