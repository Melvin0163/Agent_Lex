# Section 12. Oral evidence in contradiction of standing orders not admissible

Part of [Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946
12. Oral evidence in contradiction of standing orders not

admissible
No oral evidence having the effect of adding to or otherwise

varying or contradicting standing order as finally certified under this Act

shall be admitted in any court.
