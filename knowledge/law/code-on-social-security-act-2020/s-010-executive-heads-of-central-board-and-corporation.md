# Section 10. Executive Heads of Central Board and Corporation

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

10. Executive Heads of Central Board and Corporation.-
The Central Provident Fund Commissioner and the Director General shall be the whole-time officer of the Central Board and the Corporation, respectively, and such officer shall not undertake any work unconnected with his office without the prior approval of the Central Government.
