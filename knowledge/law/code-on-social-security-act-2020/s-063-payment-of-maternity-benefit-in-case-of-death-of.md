# Section 63. Payment of maternity benefit in case of death of a woman

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

63. Payment of maternity benefit in case of death of a woman.-
If a woman entitled to maternity benefit or any other amount under this Chapter, dies before receiving such maternity benefit or amount, or where the employer is liable for maternity benefit under the second proviso to sub-section (3) of section 60, the employer shall pay such benefit or amount to the person nominated by the woman in the notice given under section 62 and in case there is no such nominee, to her legal representative.
