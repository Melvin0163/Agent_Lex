# Section 28. All employees to be insured

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

28. All employees to be insured.-
(1) Subject to the provisions of this Code, every employee in an establishment to which this Chapter applies shall be insured in such manner whether electronically or otherwise, as may be prescribed by the Central Government.
(2) An employee whether insured or insurable under sub-section (1) in respect of whom contributions are or were payable and who is by reason thereof, entitled to any of the benefits provided under this Chapter, shall be called "Insured Person".
