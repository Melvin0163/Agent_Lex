# Section 98. Power to submit cases

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

98. Power to submit cases.-
A competent authority may, if he thinks fit, submit any question of law for the decision of the High Court and, if he does so, shall decide the question in conformity with such decision.
