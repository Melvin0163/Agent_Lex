# Section 71. Duties of employer

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

71. Duties of employer.-
An abstract of the provisions of this Chapter and the rules relating thereto in the language or languages of the locality shall be exhibited in a conspicuous place by the employer in every part of the establishment in which women are employed.
