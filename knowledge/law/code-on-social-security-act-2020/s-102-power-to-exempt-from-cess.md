# Section 102. Power to exempt from cess

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

102.Power to exempt from cess.-
Notwithstanding anything contained in this Chapter, the appropriate Government may, by notification, exempt any employer or class of employers in a State from the payment of cess payable under this Chapter where such cess is already levied and payable under any corresponding law in force in that State.
