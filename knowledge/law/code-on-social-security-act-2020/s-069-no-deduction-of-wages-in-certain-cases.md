# Section 69. No deduction of wages in certain cases

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

69. No deduction of wages in certain cases.-
No deduction from the normal and usual daily wages of a woman entitled to maternity benefit under the provisions of this Chapter shall be made by reason only of-
(a) the nature of work assigned to her by virtue of the provisions contained in section 59; or
(b) breaks for nursing the child allowed to her under the provisions of section 66.
