# Section 150. Power to frame schemes

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

150. Power to frame schemes.-
The appropriate Government may, subject to the condition of previous publication, frame schemes not inconsistent with this Code, for the purposes of giving effect to the provisions thereof.
