# Section 86. Remedies of employer against stranger

Part of [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

86. Remedies of employer against stranger.-
Where an employee has recovered compensation in respect of any injury caused under circumstances creating a legal liability of some person other than the person by whom the compensation was paid to pay damages in respect thereof, the person by whom the compensation was paid and any person who has been called on to pay an indemnity under section 85 shall be entitled to be indemnified by the person so liable to pay damages as aforesaid.
