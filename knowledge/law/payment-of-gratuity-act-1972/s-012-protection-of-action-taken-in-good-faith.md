# Section 12. Protection of action taken in good faith

Part of [Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
12.Protection of action taken in good faith.-
No suit or other legal proceeding shall lie against the

controlling authority or any other person in respect of anything which is in

good faith done or intended to be done under this Act or any rule or order made

thereunder.
