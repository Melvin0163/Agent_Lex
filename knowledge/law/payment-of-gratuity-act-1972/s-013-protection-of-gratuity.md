# Section 13. Protection of gratuity

Part of [Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
13.Protection of gratuity.-
No gratuity payable under this Act shall be liable to attachment

in execution of any decree or order of any civil, revenue or criminal court.
