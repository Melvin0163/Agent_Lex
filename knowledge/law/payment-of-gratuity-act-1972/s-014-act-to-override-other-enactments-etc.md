# Section 14. Act to override other enactments, etc

Part of [Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972
14.Act to override other enactments, etc.-
The provisions of this Act or any rule made thereunder shall

have effect notwithstanding anything inconsistent therewith contained in any

enactment other than this Act or in any instrument or contract having effect by

virtue of any enactment other than this Act.
