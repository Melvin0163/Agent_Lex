# Section 5. Notification of District Officer

Part of [Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013](overview.md). Status: active.

## Text

The Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013
Chapter III Constitution of

Local Complaints Committee
5. Notification of District

Officer.
The appropriate Government

may notify a District Magistrate or Additional District Magistrate or the

Collector or Deputy Collector as a District Officer for every District to

exercise powers or discharge functions under this Act.
