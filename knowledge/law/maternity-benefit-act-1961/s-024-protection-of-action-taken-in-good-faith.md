# Section 24. Protection of action taken in good faith

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
24. Protection of action taken in good faith 
No suit, prosecution or other legal proceedings shall lie against

any person for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done in

pursuance of this Act or of any rule or order made thereunder.
