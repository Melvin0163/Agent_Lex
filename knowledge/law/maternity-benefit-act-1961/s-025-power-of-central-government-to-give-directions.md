# Section 25. Power of Central Government to give directions

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
25. Power of Central Government to give directions 
The Central Government may give such directions as it may deem

necessary to a State Government regarding the carrying into execution of the

provisions of this Act and the State Government shall comply with such

directions.
