# Section 26. Power to exempt establishments

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
26. Power to exempt establishments 
If the appropriate government is satisfied that having regard to

an establishment or a class of establishments providing for the grant of

benefits which are not less favorable than those provided in this Act, it is

necessary so to do, it may, by notification in the Official Gazette, exempt,

subject to such conditions and restrictions, if any, as may be specified in the

notification, the establishment or class of establishments from the operation

of all or any of the provisions of this Act or of any rule made thereunder.
