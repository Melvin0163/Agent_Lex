# Section 9. Leave for miscarriage, etc. In case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, a woman shall, on production of

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
9. Leave for miscarriage, etc. 

In case of miscarriage or medical termination of pregnancy, a

woman shall, on production of such proof as may be prescribed, be entitled to

leave with wages at the rate of maternity benefit, for a period of six weeks

immediately following the day of her miscarriage or, as the case may be, her

medical termination of pregnancy.]
