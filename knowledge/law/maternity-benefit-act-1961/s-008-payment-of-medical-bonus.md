# Section 8. Payment of medical bonus

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
8. Payment of medical bonus 
Every woman entitled to maternity benefit under this Act shall

also be entitled to receive from her employer a medical bonus, of 17[two

hundred and fifty rupees], if no pre-natal confinement and post-natal care is

provided for by the employer free of charge.
