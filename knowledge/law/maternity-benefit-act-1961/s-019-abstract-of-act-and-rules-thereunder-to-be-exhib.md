# Section 19. Abstract of Act and rules thereunder to be exhibited

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
19. Abstract of Act and rules thereunder to be exhibited 
An abstract of the provisions of this Act and the rules made

thereunder in the language or languages of the locality shall be exhibited in a

conspicuous place by the employer in every part of the establishment in which

women are employed.
