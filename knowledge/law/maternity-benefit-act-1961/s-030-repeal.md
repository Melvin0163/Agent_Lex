# Section 30. Repeal

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
30. Repeal 
On the application of this Act- 
(i) to mines, the Mines Maternity Benefit Act, 1941 (19 of

1941); and
(ii) to factories situate in the Union territory of Delhi,

Bombay Maternity Benefit Act, 1929 (Bombay Act VII of 1929); as in force in

that territory, shall stand repealed
