# Section 7. Payment of maternity benefit in case of death of a woman

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
7. Payment of maternity benefit in case of death of a

woman 
If a woman entitled to maternity benefit or any other amount

under this Act, dies before receiving such maternity benefit or amount, or

where the employer is liable for maternity benefit under the second proviso to

sub-section (3) of section 5, the employer shall pay such benefit or amount to

the person nominated by the woman in the notice given under section 6 and in

case there is no such nominee, to her legal representative.
