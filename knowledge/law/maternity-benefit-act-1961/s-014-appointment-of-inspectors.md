# Section 14. Appointment of Inspectors

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
14. Appointment of Inspectors 
The appropriate government may, by notification in the Official

Gazette, appoint such officers as it thinks fit to be Inspectors for the

purposes of this Act and may define the local limits of the jurisdiction within

which they shall exercise their functions under this Act.
