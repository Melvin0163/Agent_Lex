# Section 22. Penalty for obstructing Inspector

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
22. Penalty for obstructing Inspector 
Whoever fails to produce on demand by the Inspector any register

or document in his custody kept in pursuance of this Act or the rules made thereunder

or conceals or prevents any person from appearing before or being examined by

an Inspector shall be punishable with imprisonment which may extend to 20[one

year], or with fine which may extend to five thousand rupees, or with both.
