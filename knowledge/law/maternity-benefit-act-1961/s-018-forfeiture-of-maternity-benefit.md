# Section 18. Forfeiture of maternity benefit

Part of [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Maternity Benefit Act,1961 
18. Forfeiture of maternity benefit
If a woman works in any establishment after she has been

permitted by her employer to absent herself under the provisions of section 6

for any period during such authorized absence, she shall forfeit her claim to

the maternity benefit for such period.
