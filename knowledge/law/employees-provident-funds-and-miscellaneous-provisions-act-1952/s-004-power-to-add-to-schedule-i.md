# Section 4. Power to add to Schedule I

Part of [Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020.

## Text

Employee's Provident Funds Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952
4. Power to add to Schedule I
(1) The Central Government may, by

notification in the Official Gazette, add to Schedule I any other industry in

respect of the employees whereof it is of opinion that a provident fund scheme

should be framed under this Act, and thereupon the industry so added shall be

deemed to be an industry specified in Schedule I for the purposes of this Act.
(2) All notifications under sub-section (1)

shall be laid before Parliament, as soon as may be, after they are issued.
