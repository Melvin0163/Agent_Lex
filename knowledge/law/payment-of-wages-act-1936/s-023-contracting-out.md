# Section 23. Contracting out

Part of [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Wages, 2019.

## Text

Payment of Wages Act, 1936
23.

Contracting out
Any

contract or agreement whether made before or after the commencement of this Act

whereby an employed person relinquishes any right conferred by this Act shall

be null and void in so far as it purports to deprive him of such right.
