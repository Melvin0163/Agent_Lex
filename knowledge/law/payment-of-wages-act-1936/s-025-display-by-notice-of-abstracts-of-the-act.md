# Section 25. Display by notice of abstracts of the Act

Part of [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Wages, 2019.

## Text

Payment of Wages Act, 1936
25.

Display by notice of abstracts of the Act
The

person responsible for the payment of wages of persons employed in a factory or

an industrial or other establishment shall cause to be displayed in such

factory or industrial or other establishment a notice containing such abstracts

of this Act and of the rules made thereunder in

English and in the language of the majority of the persons employed in the

factory, or industrial or other establishment as may be prescribed.
