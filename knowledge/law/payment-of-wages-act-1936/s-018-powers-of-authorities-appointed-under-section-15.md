# Section 18. Powers of authorities appointed under section 15

Part of [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Wages, 2019.

## Text

Payment of Wages Act, 1936
18.

Powers of authorities appointed under section 15
Every

authority appointed under sub-section (1) of section 15 shall have all the

powers of a civil court under the Code of Civil Procedure 1908 (5 of 1908) for

the purpose of taking evidence and of enforcing the attendance of witnesses and

compelling the production of documents and every such authority shall be deemed

to be a civil court for all the purposes of section 195 and of Chapter XXVI of

the Code of Criminal Procedure 1973 (2 of 1974).
