# Section 11. Deductions for services rendered

Part of [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Wages, 2019.

## Text

Payment of Wages Act, 1936
11.

Deductions for services rendered
A

deduction under clause (d) or clause (e) of sub-section (2) of section 7 shall

not be made from the wages of an employed person, unless the

house-accommodation amenity or service has been accepted by him as a term of

employment or otherwise and such deduction shall not exceed an amount

equivalent to the value of the house-accommodation amenity or service supplied

and in the case of deduction under the said clause (e) shall be subject to such

conditions as the State Government may impose.
