# Section 24. Application of Act to railways air transport services mines and oilfields

Part of [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Code on Wages, 2019.

## Text

Payment of Wages Act, 1936
24.

Application of Act to railways air transport services mines and oilfields
The

powers by this Act conferred upon the State Government shall, in relation to

railways air transport services mines and oilfields be powers of the Central

Government.
