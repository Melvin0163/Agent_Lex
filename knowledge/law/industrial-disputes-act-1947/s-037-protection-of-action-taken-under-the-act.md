# Section 37. Protection of action taken under the Act

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
37. Protection of action taken under

the Act
No suit, prosecution or other legal proceeding shall lie against

any person for anything which is in good faith done or intended to be done in

pursuance of this Act or any rules made thereunder.
