# Section 25. Prohibition of financial aid to illegal strikes and Lockouts

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
25. Prohibition of financial aid to

illegal strikes and Lockouts
No person shall knowingly expend or apply any money in direct

furtherance or support of any illegal strike or lock-out.
