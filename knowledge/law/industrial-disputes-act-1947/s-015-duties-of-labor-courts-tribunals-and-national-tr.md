# Section 15. Duties of Labor Courts, Tribunals and National Tribunals Where an industrial dispute has been referred to a Labor Court

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
15. Duties of Labor Courts, Tribunals and

National Tribunals

Where an industrial dispute has been referred to a Labor Court,

Tribunal or National Tribunal for adjudication, it shall hold its proceedings

expeditiously and shall, 102[within the period specified in the

order referring such industrial dispute or the further period extended under

the second proviso to sub-section (2A) of section 10], submit its award to the

appropriate government.
