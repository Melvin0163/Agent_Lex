# Section 14. Duties of courts

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
14. Duties of courts
A court shall inquire into the matters referred to it and report

thereon to the appropriate government ordinarily within a period of six months

from the commencement of its inquiry.
