# Section 28. Penalty for giving financial aid to illegal strikes and Lockouts

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
28. Penalty for giving financial aid to

illegal strikes and Lockouts
Any person who knowingly expends or applies any money in direct

furtherance or support of any illegal strike or lock-out shall be punishable

with imprisonment for a term which may extend to six months, or with fine which

may extend to one thousand rupees, or with both.
