# Section 4. Conciliation officers

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
4. Conciliation officers
(1) The appropriate. government may, by notification in

the Official Gazette, appoint such number of persons, as it thinks fit to be

conciliation officers, charged with the duty of mediating in and promoting the

settlement of industrial disputes.
(2) A conciliation officer may be appointed for a specified area

or for specified industries in a specified area or for one or more. specified

industries and either permanently or for a limited period.
