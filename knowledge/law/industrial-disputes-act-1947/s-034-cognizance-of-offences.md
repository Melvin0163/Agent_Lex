# Section 34. Cognizance of offences

Part of [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](overview.md). Status: repealed, replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020.

## Text

Industrial Disputes Act, 1947
34. Cognizance of offences
(1) No court shall take cognizance of any offence

punishable under this Act or of the abetment of any such offence, save on

complaint made by or under the authority of the appropriate government.
(2) No court inferior to that of 102[a Metropolitan

Magistrate or a Judicial Magistrate of the first class] shall try any offence

punishable under this Act.
