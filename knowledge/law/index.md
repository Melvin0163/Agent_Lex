# Indian employment law

Statutes in this bundle. Prefer **active** laws; repealed ones are kept for history.

- [Code on Social Security Act, 2020](code-on-social-security-act-2020/index.md) - active, 164 sections. Social security: provident fund, employees' state insurance, gratuity, maternity benefit, employee compensation for injury, gig and unorganised workers.
- [Code on Wages, 2019](code-on-wages-2019/index.md) - active, 69 sections. Wages: minimum wages, payment of wages and time limits, deductions and fines, overtime, equal pay regardless of gender, bonus, claims for unpaid dues.
- [Indian Contract Act, 1872](indian-contract-act-1872/index.md) - active, 75 sections. General contract law: when agreements are valid or void, free consent, restraint of trade (non-compete), compensation and penalties for breach of contract.
- [Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace (Prevention, Prohibition and Redressal) Act, 2013](sexual-harassment-of-women-at-workplace-prevention-prohibition-and-red/index.md) - active, 31 sections. Prevention of sexual harassment of women at the workplace: Internal Committee, complaints, inquiry, employer duties and penalties.
- [Employees Provident Funds and Miscellaneous Provisions Act, 1952](employees-provident-funds-and-miscellaneous-provisions-act-1952/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020), 22 sections. Provident fund and pension schemes (older law, now replaced).
- [Industrial Disputes Act, 1947](industrial-disputes-act-1947/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020), 40 sections. Industrial disputes, conciliation, strikes and lockouts (older law, now replaced).
- [Industrial Employment (Standing Orders) Act, 1946](industrial-employment-standing-orders-act-1946/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Industrial Relations Code, 2020), 15 sections. Standing orders on conditions of employment (older law, now replaced).
- [Maternity Benefit Act, 1961](maternity-benefit-act-1961/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020), 30 sections. Maternity leave and benefits for women employees (older law, now replaced).
- [Minimum Wages Act, 1948](minimum-wages-act-1948/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Code on Wages, 2019), 5 sections. Fixing and paying minimum wages (older law, now replaced).
- [Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972](payment-of-gratuity-act-1972/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Code on Social Security, 2020), 15 sections. Gratuity on leaving after continuous service (older law, now replaced).
- [Payment of Wages Act, 1936](payment-of-wages-act-1936/index.md) - repealed (replaced by Code on Wages, 2019), 26 sections. Timely payment of wages and permitted deductions (older law, now replaced).
