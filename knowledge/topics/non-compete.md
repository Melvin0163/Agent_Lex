# Non-compete and restraint after leaving a job

Clauses stopping an employee from joining a competitor or starting a business after leaving.

Indian statutes describe these as agreements 'in restraint of trade'. Read Section 27 and its exception carefully; restrictions during employment and after employment can be treated differently.

Also called: non-compete, non compete, restrictive covenant, non-solicitation, restraint of trade, join a competitor, after resignation, post-employment restriction
