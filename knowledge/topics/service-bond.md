# Training bonds, service bonds and liquidated damages

Clauses requiring an employee to pay a fixed sum if they leave before a minimum period.

A sum named in a contract for breach is dealt with as compensation for breach where a penalty is stipulated (Section 74); general compensation for breach is in Section 73.

Also called: training bond, service bond, employment bond, liquidated damages, penalty clause, bond amount, breach of contract, leave before, lock-in period
