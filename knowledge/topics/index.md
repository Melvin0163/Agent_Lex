# Legal topics

Everyday employment questions mapped to the statute sections that govern them.

- [Non-compete and restraint after leaving a job](non-compete.md) - Clauses stopping an employee from joining a competitor or starting a business after leaving. Also called: non-compete, non compete, restrictive covenant, non-solicitation, restraint of trade, join a competitor.

- [Working hours and overtime pay](overtime-hours.md) - Normal working day and wages for overtime work. Also called: overtime, extra hours, working hours, OT pay, work beyond hours, long hours.
- [Equal pay and gender discrimination in wages](equal-pay.md) - No discrimination in wages or recruitment on the ground of gender. Also called: equal pay, gender pay gap, paid less than men, discrimination in salary.

- [Validity of employment agreements](contract-validity.md) - When an agreement is a valid contract and when terms are void. Also called: valid contract, void clause, free consent, unlawful, signed under pressure, unfair terms.
