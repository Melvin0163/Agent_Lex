# Final settlement of dues after resignation or termination

When wages and dues must be paid after an employee resigns, is removed, dismissed or retrenched.

The time limit for wages when employment ends is in the Code on Wages, Section 17.

Also called: full and final settlement, F&F, final settlement, dues after resignation, last salary, relieving, notice pay, pending dues, settlement delay
